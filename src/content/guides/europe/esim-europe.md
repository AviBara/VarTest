Europe has the simplest mobile setup in the world for travellers: a SIM from any EU country roams across the whole EU at no extra cost. That changes the usual advice.

## The main choice

- **Staying mostly in the EU**: buy one local prepaid SIM or eSIM in your first EU country and use it everywhere. Data allowances are generous and prices are low.
- **Short trip or many countries including non-EU ones**: a regional eSIM covering 35 to 40 European countries removes the guesswork.
- **Arriving in the UK, Switzerland, Türkiye or the Balkans first**: those are not in EU roaming. Check the plan's country list before buying.

## Countries that surprise people

| Country | EU roaming? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Switzerland | No | Often excluded; buy a Swiss SIM or a regional eSIM that lists it |
| United Kingdom | No (post-Brexit) | Some EU operators still include it, most do not |
| Norway, Iceland | Yes | Part of the EEA, included |
| Türkiye, Georgia | No | Separate local SIMs are cheap |
| Balkans (Serbia, Albania, Bosnia) | Partly | Roaming is being phased in; verify per operator |

## Fair-use limits

EU roaming has a fair-use cap on data. On very large or unlimited plans you may get a reduced allowance abroad. The operator states the roaming limit in the plan details.

## Setup checklist

1. Confirm your phone is unlocked and supports eSIM.
2. Install the eSIM at home on wifi; activate on arrival.
3. Turn off data roaming on your home SIM to avoid surprise bills.
