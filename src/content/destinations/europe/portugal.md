Portugal has become the default first stop for anyone working remotely in Europe: mild winters, fast fibre, English almost everywhere and prices that are still below Western European averages, though rising fast in Lisbon.

## Where to base yourself

- **Lisbon**: the most infrastructure and the highest rents. Look beyond Chiado and Príncipe Real to Arroios, Campo de Ourique or across the river in Almada.
- **Porto**: cheaper, more compact, a strong café and coworking scene. Winters are wetter.
- **Algarve (Lagos, Faro, Tavira)**: beaches, surf and a large expat community. Quiet and affordable in winter.
- **Madeira**: year-round mild weather and a dedicated nomad village programme in Ponta do Sol.

## Connectivity

MEO, Vodafone and NOS all offer good coverage. Prepaid SIMs are cheap; an EU-wide eSIM works fine and avoids the shop visit.

## Visas

Non-EU citizens can stay 90 days in any 180 under Schengen rules. For longer, Portugal's D8 visa targets remote workers earning at least four times the local minimum wage. Processing takes months, so apply early.
