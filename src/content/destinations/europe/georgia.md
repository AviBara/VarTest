Georgia sits at the edge of Europe with one of the most generous entry policies anywhere: a full year visa-free for most Western passports. Add cheap rent, excellent food and mountains two hours from the capital, and it is easy to see why the nomad community keeps growing.

## Tbilisi

The capital has everything a remote worker needs: fast fibre, coworkings in Vake and Vera, and a café culture that welcomes laptops. Old Town and Sololaki are the most atmospheric; Vake and Saburtalo are more residential and quieter.

## Beyond the capital

- **Kazbegi (Stepantsminda)**: alpine scenery, the Gergeti Trinity Church, day-hikes.
- **Kakheti**: the wine region, with family wineries and guesthouses.
- **Batumi**: Black Sea coast, a subtropical climate, cheap in the off-season.
- **Svaneti**: remote valleys, medieval towers and serious trekking in summer.

## Connectivity

Magti and Silknet have good coverage across the country, including most mountain towns. SIMs are cheap and sold at the airport.

## Practical notes

Georgia is not in the EU or Schengen, so days here do not count against your Schengen allowance. That makes it a common stop for people who need to reset their 90-day clock.
