Spain offers the widest choice of lifestyles in Southern Europe, from Madrid's late-night energy to Valencia's beach-and-bike routine. Costs vary as much as the cities do.

## Picking a city

- **Madrid**: the capital, great transport, no beach. Best food scene per euro in the country.
- **Barcelona**: sea, architecture, the biggest international community. Also the most crowded and the tightest rental market.
- **Valencia**: the value pick. Beach, flat cycling city, rents around 30% below Barcelona.
- **Málaga and Seville**: Andalusian sun, lower prices, growing tech and nomad scenes.
- **Canary Islands**: winter sun, a favourite for long stays between November and March.

## Connectivity and transport

Movistar, Orange and Vodafone cover the whole country; low-cost brands like Digi and Lowi are much cheaper for locals. High-speed trains connect Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, Seville and Málaga in under three hours.

## Visas and residence

Spain's digital nomad visa allows up to one year initially (renewable to five) for remote workers with a foreign employer or clients. It comes with a reduced flat tax rate for eligible applicants. Requirements change, so verify on the consulate site before applying.
