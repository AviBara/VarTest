Two hours from most of Western Europe by budget airline, Morocco is the easiest way to add Africa to a trip. It also works well for longer stays: a growing number of remote workers base themselves in Taghazout, Essaouira or Marrakech through the winter.

## Where to go

- **Marrakech**: the medina, the souks, riads and day trips to the Atlas. Chaotic and worth it.
- **Fes**: the most intact medieval medina in the Arab world. Less touristy than Marrakech.
- **Essaouira**: laid-back Atlantic town, wind, seafood and a calmer pace.
- **Taghazout and Tamraght**: surf villages near Agadir with coliving spaces and year-round sun.
- **Chefchaouen**: the blue town in the Rif mountains.
- **Sahara (Merzouga)**: dunes, camel treks and desert camps; go in spring or autumn.

## Connectivity

Maroc Telecom, Orange and Inwi all sell cheap prepaid SIMs; Maroc Telecom has the widest coverage in the mountains and desert. 4G is good in cities; fibre is common in newer apartments.

## Practical notes

Bargaining is normal in souks and for taxis without meters. Dress modestly outside beach towns. Friday is the main prayer day and some shops close around midday. Alcohol is available but not everywhere.
