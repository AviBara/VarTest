South Africa is the most accessible entry point to the continent: English is everywhere, the infrastructure is solid and a weak rand makes it excellent value for anyone earning in dollars or euros.

## Cape Town

Consistently ranked among the best cities for remote work. Table Mountain, beaches on both the Atlantic and False Bay sides, and a wine region 45 minutes away. Base yourself in Sea Point, Green Point, Gardens or the City Bowl. Coworking spaces are plentiful.

## Beyond Cape Town

- **Garden Route (Knysna, Plettenberg Bay, Wilderness)**: a week-long coastal road trip east of Cape Town.
- **Kruger National Park**: self-drive safari at a fraction of the cost of guided lodges. Best in the dry winter months.
- **Johannesburg**: the economic hub, rarely a tourist stop, but a strong tech scene and the gateway to Kruger.
- **Drakensberg**: mountains and hiking, cool in summer.

## Connectivity

Vodacom and MTN have the best coverage. Load shedding (scheduled power cuts) has eased but still happens; choose accommodation with backup power if you need to be online for work.

## Safety

Petty crime is the main risk in cities. Use ride-hailing apps rather than walking at night, keep valuables out of sight in cars, and ask locally which areas to avoid. Rural areas and the Garden Route are relaxed.
